    Oracle’s exact allowance ↗
    ↗
    Put the heavy work in the right place.
    The gateway forwards inference to model providers. A remote harness moves file and shell work off the laptop, but needs its own memory and CPU budget.
    Will the whole stack fit on the Micro?
    Not established. The inspected self-host guide asks for about 2 GB free RAM and its environment template defaults to a 2048 MB heap. A 1 GB Micro plus swap is an experiment, not a validated deployment. Keep dsh local and measure actual gateway memory.
    OCI also lists an Always Free A1 allowance of 2 OCPUs / 12 GB total at this research snapshot. Check capacity, architecture compatibility and measured usage before choosing a host.
    Runtime guidance ↗
    ∞
    Always Free is a price, not an uptime promise.
    Home-region capacity can be unavailable. Oracle may reclaim idle free instances. Persistent storage, a supervised process and backups matter for unattended work.
    $
    Free routes need a real free pool.
    OmniRoute doesn’t make paid models free. Connect eligible accounts, exclude paid targets, and honour provider limits. When every free route is exhausted, the request must wait or fail.

    THE QUIETLY IMPORTANT PART
    No public
    front door needed.
    Reach your gateway over Tailscale. Restrict the app to the tailnet, keep public application ingress closed, and use tailnet policy plus gateway authentication.
    Tailscale usually needs no inbound firewall opening. It still needs outbound connectivity; if NAT prevents a direct path, DERP relays encrypted traffic. This protects the device-to-gateway leg—provider requests still leave via HTTPS.
